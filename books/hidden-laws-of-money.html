The Hidden Laws of Money

The rules that quietly decide who wins and who stays broke.

Most money advice tells you what to do: budget more, invest earlier, cut the lattes. The Hidden Laws of Money asks a different question: why do two people with the same income, the same information, and the same opportunities end up in completely different financial places?

Eric Coste lays out ten laws that explain the invisible mechanics behind who builds wealth almost by accident and who stays stuck despite working twice as hard. It's not a spreadsheet book. It's a mindset book with teeth, written in short chapters you can actually finish, each ending with a reflection you can act on the same day.

What's Inside

  • 10 laws covering identity, risk, timing, leverage, and the stories people tell themselves about money
  • A companion workbook with frameworks, reflection questions, and action steps for every law
  • Real, grounded examples instead of hustle-culture platitudes
