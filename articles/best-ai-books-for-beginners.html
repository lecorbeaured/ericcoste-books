Best AI Books for Beginners

No coding background, no data science degree, no jargon you have to look up every other page.

Most "intro to AI" books are secretly written for people who already have some technical footing. The five below aren't. Each assumes nothing except curiosity, and each tells you upfront what kind of beginner it's for, because "beginner who wants to understand AI" and "beginner who wants to make money with AI" need different books.

01
Best Overall Starting Point

Co-Intelligence

by Ethan Mollick

Written specifically to get a non-technical reader comfortable using AI tools well, not just understanding them academically. Practical exercises, no prerequisite knowledge, and it doesn't talk down to you.

02
Best for Making Money, Not Just Learning

AI Is Making People Money. Here's How.

by Eric Coste

Purpose-built for beginners who don't want to just understand AI, they want to turn it into income within 30 days. Zero coding, zero audience, zero investor money required. Eight income models, copy-paste outreach scripts, and a week-by-week action plan.

03
Best for a Laugh While You Learn

You Look Like a Thing and I Love You

by Janelle Shane

Explains how machine learning actually works using genuinely funny examples (an AI trying and failing to name paint colors, write pickup lines, and generate recipes). Deceptively educational.

04
Best for the Business Angle

Prediction Machines

by Ajay Agrawal, Joshua Gans, and Avi Goldfarb

If your entry point into AI is "what does this mean for my job or my business," this is the most beginner-friendly economics explanation available, no finance degree needed.

05
Best for the Big Picture

Genius Makers

by Cade Metz

A narrative history rather than a how-to, useful if you want context for how we got here before you start using the tools yourself. No technical knowledge assumed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best AI books for beginners right now?
Co-Intelligence by Ethan Mollick is the strongest general starting point. If your goal is specifically to earn income from AI rather than just understand it, start with AI Is Making People Money. Here's How. instead.
Do I need to know how to code to read these?
No. None of the five books above require any coding, and none assume prior AI or data science knowledge.
Which book should I read first if I want to start earning from AI within a month?
AI Is Making People Money. Here's How. is built around a 30-day action plan for exactly that, aimed at people with no audience or portfolio yet.
